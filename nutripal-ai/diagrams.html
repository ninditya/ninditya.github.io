Diagram 05

User Flow & Journey Map

Eight stages from first launch to retention, with what the member does, what the system does behind it, and where the experience is currently thin.

# Stage Member does System / AI does Friction today
01 Sign in One tap on “Sign in with Google (Demo Mode)”. signInAnonymously(), row created in users, onboarding status checked. Anonymous session cannot be recovered on another device.
02 Onboarding Enters sex, age, weight, height; ticks health markers and allergies. BMI computed live; Mifflin-St Jeor BMR × 1.2 gives the calorie target, ±500 kcal by BMI band. Activity level is fixed at sedentary — no goal or timeline question.
03 Daily check Opens Today, reads energy balance, taps hydration. Totals recomputed from consumed meals; realtime channel keeps the figure live across devices. Nothing to do when the day is empty — no next action offered.
04 Plan Adds a meal by name, or leaves macros at zero. analyzeTextMeal estimates calories, macros, health score, instructions and allergen conflicts. No copy-yesterday or repeat-weekly shortcut.
05 Log by photo Shoots or uploads the plate, reviews the breakdown, syncs. Gemini Vision identifies the dish, estimates portions, audits allergens, writes the meal and increments the day. No portion correction step; the estimate is accepted as-is.
06 Discover Runs ML curation, pantry recipes or a predictive day plan. Profile + remaining calories are scored against the catalogue; the winner is written to meals before the deep link opens. The delivery catalogue is a four-item fixture, not a live feed.
07 Sync & review Checks the weekly trend, syncs the watch, edits the profile. Burn over 700 kcal unlocks the recovery-plan synthesis for tomorrow. Wearable data is mocked, so the trigger is not yet trustworthy.
08 Retain Comes back tomorrow — or does not. Meal reminders are stored per meal (reminder_enabled) but not yet delivered. No streaks, no push, no weekly digest — the retention loop is unbuilt.

Diagram 07

Business Model Canvas

Indonesia-first, mobile-web, freemium. The delivery integration is what separates NutriPal from a generic tracker: it converts a logging habit into a transaction.

Key Partners
  • GrabFood & GoFood merchants
  • Healthy-meal kitchens (CookBuddy model)
  • Google AI Studio / Gemini
  • Supabase
  • Clinical nutritionists for content review
  • Wearable platforms — Health Connect, Apple Health
Key Activities
  • Prompt & schema engineering for the five flows
  • Indonesian food-recognition accuracy work
  • Catalogue ingestion from delivery platforms
  • Retention loops: reminders, streaks, digests
Value Propositions

Log a plate of Indonesian food in one photo — and get told what to eat next.

  • Photo → calories, macros, health score, allergen audit
  • Advice that respects diabetes, hypertension and allergies
  • Recommendations you can actually order, from apps you already use
  • Recipes from what is already in the kitchen
  • One number to look at: energy balance for today
Customer Relationships
  • Self-serve, one-tap onboarding
  • Daily AI insight as the habit hook
  • Pro tier for unlimited analyses
  • In-app nutritionist review (roadmap)
Customer Segments
  • Urban office workers 24–40 — order in most days, want control without cooking
  • Members with a health marker — diabetes, hypertension, allergies
  • Gym-going young adults — protein and recovery focused
  • B2B later: corporate wellness, insurers, clinics
Key Resources
  • Indonesian dish + portion reference data
  • Typed Genkit flow library
  • Longitudinal member logs (with consent)
  • Small product + AI team
Channels
  • Mobile web (installable PWA)
  • TikTok / Instagram health creators
  • Gym and clinic partnerships
  • Placement inside delivery-app promos
Cost Structure
  • Gemini inference — the dominant variable cost, one call per photo
  • Supabase hosting, storage for meal images, egress
  • Engineering and prompt maintenance
  • Nutrition content review and localisation
  • Acquisition: creator fees and partnership rev-share
Revenue Streams — options to test
1 · Pro subscription
Free tier caps photo analyses per day; Pro unlocks unlimited scans, meal-plan synthesis and history. The “Pro Member” badge already ships in the UI.
2 · Delivery commission
Rev-share on orders placed through curated recommendations — the highest-intent moment in the product and the least intrusive to the member.
3 · CookBuddy fulfilment
Margin on prepared meals cooked to the synthesised plan; already modelled as a source value in the schema.
4 · B2B seats
Corporate wellness and insurer programmes buying aggregate, anonymised adherence reporting.

Recommendation: launch on 1 + 2. Both are already reachable from today's build; 3 and 4 need operations and a data-governance story first.
