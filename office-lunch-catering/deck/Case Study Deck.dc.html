The problem

01 · The problem

Three failures, one process

Wrong quantities

The headcount is done by hand each morning against a chat thread, so the order never matches who is actually in the office.

Preferences nowhere

Allergies, halal, vegetarian and calorie needs are never recorded in any structured form — they survive as things people remember.

Month-end by chat history

Matching a month of orders against the vendor’s claim takes days, and the report Finance needs is assembled separately.

01 · The problem

Six actors, two of them systems

Employee

Keeps a dietary profile, makes one choice a day, sees their own consumption record.

Office Admin · primary persona

Owns vendors, menus, the daily count, reconciliation and payment — the operational and financial risk.

Vendor and Finance

Estimate, final order and delta go out; the invoice and payment report come back as PDF and Excel.

Scheduler and HRIS

One runs the scheduled jobs, the other supplies daily attendance through a webhook.

The system

02 · The system

One loop, on a clock

The solid mark is the moment the day becomes final. Every time on this line is configurable without a deploy.

02 · The system

Two mechanisms

Attendance decides eligibility

WFO and overtime get a portion; WFH, leave and business trips are skipped automatically. HRIS is the source of truth, with a manual Admin override as the fallback — so the count follows who is present, not how many people are employed.

Eliminate, then rank

Allergen, halal and vegetarian rules remove menus entirely. Only what remains is scored, on calorie distance to the employee’s target and variety across the week. Rule-based and auditable; machine learning waits for three to six months of ratings.

Build and priority

03 · Build and priority

The four screens that carry it

Pick menu

Countdown to cut-off, ranked cards that say why they fit, change or cancel in one tap.

Dietary profile

The input to elimination — treated as specific personal data, not a preference nicety.

Daily dashboard

Orders against attendance, cut-off countdown, and the delivery gaps already queued.

Reconciliation

Delivered × master price against the vendor claim; only the mismatched days need a person.

03 · Build and priority

16

tables — fourteen domain, plus the audit trail and notifications. Two columns protect history: the order row freezes the price it was placed at, and the invoice line keeps ordered quantity beside delivered quantity.

03 · Build and priority

Guardrails

  • Locking and aggregation complete in under a minute for 1,000 employees.
  • Allergy and restriction data is specific personal data under UU PDP 27/2022 — encrypted in transit and at rest, never written to application logs.
  • Order changes, attendance overrides and billing adjustments are written to an audit trail, retained two years.
  • Attendance sync is idempotent and fixed to WIB; every automated job has a manual path.
03 · Build and priority

Priority

Must

Auth, elimination filter, menu master, cut-off ordering, attendance eligibility, vendor sends, reconciliation.

Should

HRIS webhook, ranked recommendations, auto-assign, consumption recap, payment history, WhatsApp gateway.

Could

Cost per department, menu ratings, deeper nutrition tracking, budget alerts, vendor self-service.

Won’t · this phase

Employee-paid checkout, a vendor marketplace, native apps, ML recommendations, chatbot ordering.
